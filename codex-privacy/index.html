Codex privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Codex doesn't collect personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

What you write stays yours. Your entries, photos, handwritten pages, quotes and settings are stored on your devices and in your private iCloud account, which only you control. The developer can't read them.

On-device processing. Apple Intelligence, dictation and handwriting recognition run on your device. Your entries are not sent to any other service.

Nothing else leaves your device. Codex makes no network connection of its own. The only data that leaves your device is the iCloud sync that Apple runs for your own account.

Feedback. If you send TestFlight feedback or a crash report, Apple passes it to the developer, with any screenshot you chose to include. A report you share from Report a problem goes only where you send it.

Children. Codex is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
